MODERATOR: Welcome everyone. Tonight the panel debates raising the minimum wage.
DR. SMITH: I support raising it. Full-time workers should not live in poverty.
Studies of earlier increases show only a small effect on employment.
MR. JONES: I disagree with that. Small businesses run on thin margins.
A higher wage floor means they cut hours or lay people off.
DR. SMITH: The studies I cited looked at exactly those businesses. They found very few layoffs.
MS. PATEL: I would go further than Dr. Smith. Higher pay also means workers spend more locally.
That helps the same small businesses Mr. Jones is worried about.
MR. JONES: Spending only rises if people keep their jobs. Many of my members would not.
MODERATOR: Let us hear from the audience. Yes, the gentleman at the back.
AUDIENCE: How many of the studies were done in rural areas?
MS. PATEL: Several were. The results were similar there.
MR. JONES: I still oppose a rise this large. A gradual increase would be far safer.
DR. SMITH: A gradual increase is a reasonable compromise, and I would accept it.
MODERATOR: Thank you all. That concludes tonight's debate.
