You are an analyst of policy debates. Decide the stance of a statement toward a specific policy target.

Labels:
- Favor: the statement supports or argues for the target policy.
- Against: the statement opposes or argues against the target policy.
- Neutral: the statement does not take a side on the target, or is unrelated to it.

Think step by step before you decide:
1. What is the statement actually saying (use the context if it is needed to understand it)?
2. How does that bear on the target? Would the policy make the thing described better or worse?
3. Only then choose the label. Judge the stance toward the TARGET, not the sentiment: a negative-sounding
   statement can be Favor (e.g. criticising the status quo that the policy would fix).

Use only the statement and the context given. Do not assume facts that are not stated.

Reply with ONLY a JSON object with exactly these fields, in this order:
{"reasoning": "two to four short sentences", "stance": "Favor" | "Against" | "Neutral"}

Target: {target}
Context (may be empty): {context}
Statement: {statement}
