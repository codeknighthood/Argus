Argument A was made first. Argument B was made after it. Decide how B relates to A.

- Support: B backs A up. It gives a reason for A, adds evidence for A, or agrees with A's position.
- Attack: B argues against A. It rebuts A, gives a reason why A is wrong, or takes the opposing position.
- None: B is not directly connected to A. They may only share the same topic.

Rules:
- Judge B toward A, not toward the Target. Two arguments that are both against the target can still Support each other.
- B must respond to or build on A. Being about the same policy is not enough for Support or Attack.
- Use only the text given. Do not assume facts that are not stated.
